STEMGRADS Scraper Navigation Steps
Step 1: Visit Homepage
URL: http://data.uis.unesco.org/
Wait for page to load
Step 2: Click "Browse data"
Click the "Browse data" link in the top navigation
Goes to: /browser
Step 3: Click "Education" category
On the browser page, click "Education" to expand it
Then click "Other Policy Relevant Indicators - Education"

we are supposed to search 
 Percentage of graduates by field of education (tertiary education) 


Step 4: Click "Graduates" in left sidebar
In the left sidebar, click "Graduates"
OR search for: "Percentage of graduates by field of education"
Step 5: Expand "Distribution of graduates by field of study"
Click to expand this section (shows 13 results)
Step 6: Select the STEM indicator
Find and click: "Percentage of graduates from Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics programmes in tertiary education, both sexes (%)"
You'll see "Indicator added to the selection"
Step 7: Click "View data"
After indicator is selected, click "View data" link
Step 8: Set Time Range
Expand "Time range" section in left sidebar
Drag slider to full range (min to max years)
Step 9: Download CSV
Click "Download filtered data" button (bottom right)
Select "CSV" format
Click "Download"
Step 10: Process downloaded file
CSV has columns: indicatorId, geoUnit, year, value, qualifier, magnitude
Parse and update master data
